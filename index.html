Is It an Idol?

A Bible question game for 2nd graders
Choose A. It's an idol! or B. Not an idol!

Question 1 of 12Follow God first ❤️

Bonus Discussion Questions

  1. Can a toy become an idol? How?
  2. Can good grades become an idol?
  3. Can your friends become idols?
  4. Is playing video games a sin? When might it become too important?
  5. Can money become an idol even if you aren't rich?
  6. Can something good become an idol?
  7. Why did the Israelites worship idols instead of God?
  8. What should we do when we realize something is more important to us than God?

Remember

Good things like toys, games, and sports are not automatically idols. But when we put something above God, it can become an idol. We all sin, and Jesus died and rose again so we can be forgiven and brought back to God.

Teacher tip: Read each question aloud, let students choose A or B, then discuss the explanation together.
